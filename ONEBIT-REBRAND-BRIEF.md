# Onebit — Rebrand & Visual Identity Brief

> A design brief prepared to be fed to a generative design assistant (e.g. "Claude design").
> It defines a **new** brand direction for Onebit: logo concept, colour system, typography,
> and a landing-page one-pager — each with the reasoning behind the choice so the rationale
> can be carried into execution. Nothing here changes product copy or functionality; it is a
> visual + brand language specification.

---

## 1. Who Onebit is (the foundation everything is built on)

Onebit is an African-born technology company operating as a single hub with **three connected arms**:

| Arm | What it does | One-word idea |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| **Build** | Designs, engineers and ships intelligent enterprise systems — data platforms, AI, credit intelligence, HR, inventory, forecasting, automation. | *Systems* |
| **Learn** | Cohort-based, job-ready tech education for individuals and corporate teams. | *People* |
| **Bridge** | Matches vetted, Onebit-trained talent to real industry roles. | *Opportunity* |

- **Tagline today:** *Build. Learn. Grow. Lead.*
- **Mission:** Build intelligent systems, develop future-ready people, and unlock equitable opportunities.
- **Vision:** A talent-driven African innovation economy powering global industries.
- **Values:** People-first innovation · Executional excellence · Learning obsession · Integrity & trust.
- **Proof points already in the product:** 120+ enterprise launches, 4,500+ learners trained, 65+ global partners, roles filled across 10+ regions, 96% learner satisfaction.
- **Home base:** Abuja, Nigeria — serving teams across Africa and beyond.

**The brand must therefore hold three things in tension at once:**
1. **Engineered intelligence** (we build serious enterprise/AI systems → credibility, precision, trust).
2. **Human growth & opportunity** (we develop people and open doors → warmth, optimism, accessibility).
3. **Africa-first ambition with global standards** (proudly local, world-class in execution).

### Brand personality (the dials)
- Intelligent, not cold.
- Confident, not corporate-stiff.
- Optimistic and human, not naïve.
- Premium and precise, not exclusive or intimidating.
- Energetic, not loud.

The name itself is the strongest creative asset: **"Onebit" = one bit — the smallest, fundamental unit of information.** It's the *seed* from which systems, skills and careers are built. The whole identity should ladder back to this single idea: **one small unit → meaningful systems, capable people, real opportunity.**

## 2. Logo direction

**Goal:** Replace the current logo with a mark that (a) encodes "the bit / the unit," (b) hints at the three arms, and (c) scales perfectly from favicon to billboard.

### 2.1 The core idea
A single **"bit cell"** — a soft, rounded square (a pixel / unit / building block) — that visibly **branches or connects into three nodes**. One unit becomes Build, Learn, Bridge. It simultaneously reads as:
- a **pixel / bit** (technology, the unit of information),
- a **seed / node** (growth, beginnings),
- a **connector / bridge** (three points linked).

### 2.2 Logo system (ask the design tool to produce all of these)
1. **Symbol (the "Onebit mark")** — a rounded-square bit cell with three connected nodes radiating from it (a tiny constellation/circuit). Must work as a standalone app icon and favicon at 16px.
2. **Primary wordmark** — `onebit` set in **lowercase** for an approachable, modern, humanist feel. The dot of a letter (or a counter) is replaced by the **bit cell** so the symbol lives inside the word.
3. **Lockups** — horizontal (symbol + wordmark) and stacked (symbol above wordmark). Optional arm-suffix lockups: `onebit · build`, `onebit · learn`, `onebit · bridge`, where the suffix takes that arm's accent colour (see §3).
4. **Monochrome + reversed** — solid ink on light, solid white on dark, and a single-colour version for embroidery/stamping.

### 2.3 Construction principles
- **Geometric but soft:** rounded corners (not hard tech edges, not fully circular) — mirrors "engineered yet human."
- **Even weight, generous width** — no tall/condensed forms (consistent with the typography rule below).
- **One memorable gesture** — the "one unit → three nodes" move is the whole story; resist extra detail.

### 2.4 Don'ts
- No literal lightbulbs, globes-of-Africa clichés, gears, or generic "AI swirls."
- No gradients inside the symbol at small sizes (keep it solid for legibility; gradient is for large/marketing use only).
- No outline-only mark — it must hold up as a solid silhouette.

**Why this direction:** It is ownable (built from the literal meaning of the name), it tells the three-arm story without words, and a solid geometric unit is the most scalable, future-proof form for a company whose products live on screens.

## 3. Colour system

### 3.1 Strategy & reasoning
We move **off the current single-teal identity** toward a **disciplined, story-driven palette**:

- **One master brand colour** carries 90% of the identity for coherence.
- **Each arm gets its own accent**, so Build/Learn/Bridge are instantly recognisable and the "three connected arms" story is visible in colour — a meaningful system, not decoration.
- **A warm action colour** drives energy and conversions.
- **A deep ink neutral** anchors everything in premium, intelligent calm.

**Master brand = Indigo.**
*Why indigo?* Indigo/violet reads as **intelligence, depth, focus and premium technology** — it's the colour the modern AI/データ category gravitates to because it signals "thinking machines" without feeling cold or clinical. Crucially it is **distinct from the teal Onebit uses now** (a genuine rebrand) while still feeling trustworthy and credible to enterprise buyers. It also provides the strongest possible contrast partner for a warm gold accent.

**Action / energy = Gold-Amber.**
*Why amber-gold?* It is the colour of **opportunity, prosperity, optimism and sunlight** — the human, hopeful, "doors opening" side of Onebit (Learn + Bridge). Against deep indigo it creates a high-energy, high-contrast pairing that makes calls-to-action unmissable. It also nods to Africa-first warmth without resorting to cliché.

**Arm accents:** Build = **Indigo** (the master, since systems are the core engineering arm), Learn = **Emerald/Green** (growth, knowledge, "go," progress), Bridge = **Gold-Amber** (opportunity, connection, the warm "handshake").
*Why green for Learn?* Growth and learning are universally green; it reads as progress and positivity and balances the cooler indigo.

**Neutral = Ink (blue-black) + cool slate.**
*Why a blue-black rather than pure black/grey?* A slightly blue "ink" feels engineered and premium, harmonises with indigo, and is softer on the eye than #000 for large dark surfaces and dark mode.

### 3.3 Signature gradient (marketing/large surfaces only)
`linear-gradient(135deg, #392FA6 0%, #5A4FE0 55%, #13B97A 100%)` with optional **gold sparks** (`#F5A623`) as small glints. Use for hero backdrops, large headlines (clipped text), and the visual panels — **never** for body text or inside the small logo.

### 3.4 Light & dark, and accessibility (WCAG AA is mandatory)
- **Light theme:** `paper-50` background, `white` cards, `ink-900` headings, `slate-700` body, `indigo-600` links, `gold-500` CTAs **with `ink-950` text on them**.
- **Dark theme:** `ink-950` background, `ink-900` cards, `white`/`slate-100` text, `indigo-300`/`gold-400` accents, glows allowed.
- **Rules:** Gold always carries **dark (ink) text**, never white (gold + white fails contrast). Indigo-600 and darker pass AA for body text on white. Reserve red strictly for errors. Maintain ≥ 4.5:1 for text, ≥ 3:1 for large text/UI.

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 The rule you set: **no tall / condensed fonts**
We choose typefaces with a **generous x-height and wide, open proportions** — they feel friendly, legible and confident, and they read well to both enterprise buyers and first-time learners. We avoid condensed, narrow, or tall-display faces.

### 4.2 Recommended pairing (primary)
| Role | Typeface | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| **Display / Headings** | **Sora** | A geometric-humanist sans with an **engineered, modern feel** ("build intelligent systems") that stays **wide and open**, never tall or condensed. Distinctive at large sizes without being quirky. Free (Google Fonts). |
| **Body / UI** | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | Warm, humanist, exceptionally legible with a **tall x-height and round, friendly forms** — it carries the "human, education-first" side of Onebit and reads beautifully at small sizes. Free (Google Fonts). |
| **Mono / Accent** | **JetBrains Mono** (keep) or **Geist Mono** | A monospaced accent reinforces the *"bit / engineered"* idea. Use sparingly for code, data labels, stat figures, eyebrow tags and the `01 / 02 / 03` step counters. |

**Why this pairing works:** Sora (engineered, geometric) + Plus Jakarta Sans (warm, humanist) literally expresses Onebit's core tension — *intelligent systems built for people*. Both are wide and round (your "no tall fonts" requirement), both are open-source with full weight ranges, and the mono adds a precise "tech texture" without overwhelming.

### 4.3 Alternative pairing (if a softer, more neutral feel is preferred)
- **Headings:** **Manrope** (modern, geometric, big x-height, very neutral-professional).
- **Body:** **Inter** (the current body font — ultra-legible, safe, neutral).
- **Mono:** **JetBrains Mono**.
Use this if the brand should lean more "clean enterprise SaaS" than "characterful."

### 4.4 Type scale & usage (suggested, responsive)
| Style | Font / Weight | Size (desktop) | Notes |

**Typographic do's:** sentence case for most UI; UPPERCASE only for short mono eyebrows; generous line-height for body; never letter-space the body.

## 5. Visual language (supporting system)

- **Shape:** rounded corners (cards ~16–20px, buttons pill or 12px), echoing the soft "bit cell" of the logo.
- **Motif:** the **node/grid** — subtle dotted grids, connected dots, and "three-node" constellations as section accents and backgrounds (ties to logo + the three arms). Keep at low opacity so content leads.
- **Cards:** light theme = white cards with soft shadows and a 1px slate border; on feature/showcase sections, **deep ink cards** with a thin indigo→emerald glow border for a premium, "spotlight" pop.
- **Imagery:** real African teams + learners + product UI, blended with abstract isometric "system" renders (dashboards, nodes, flows) tinted toward the indigo/emerald palette. Authentic over stock-generic.
- **Iconography:** consistent 1.5–2px stroke, rounded joins, single accent colour per context.
- **Motion (purposeful, subtle):** soft fade/translate reveals on scroll, magnetic/spotlight hovers on cards, auto-advancing showcases with progress bars, gentle gradient drift on hero. **Always honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.**
- **Tone of voice:** confident, clear, human, action-led. Short sentences. "We build, we teach, we connect." Proudly African, globally credible. Avoid jargon-stacking.

## 6. Landing page — one-pager

A single, scroll-told story. Top-to-bottom spec for the homepage in the new brand. (Light theme by default, with ink "spotlight" sections for rhythm.)

> **Layout principle:** alternate light (paper) sections with occasional deep-ink feature sections to create rhythm; indigo leads, gold punctuates (CTAs), emerald signals Learn/growth.

1. **Top nav (sticky, translucent)**
   - New **onebit** wordmark (lowercase) at left; links: Build · Learn · Bridge · About · Contact; right: theme toggle + **gold "Book a Call"** pill.
   - On scroll: blurs to frosted `ink-900`/white with a hairline border.

2. **Hero** *(paper background, subtle node grid + indigo→emerald gradient glow)*
   - Eyebrow (mono): `ONEBIT HUB`.
   - Display headline (Sora 700, gradient-clipped key phrase): **"Build. Learn. Grow. Lead."** + a one-line promise: *"One unit of technology, the systems it builds, the people it grows, and the opportunities it opens."*
   - Sub-paragraph (Plus Jakarta) — short value statement.
   - Buttons: **primary gold** "Get Started with Onebit" + **secondary indigo-outline** "Explore Our Work" + a small ghost "play" for the demo.
   - **Right side:** an interactive **live demo reel** card (deep-ink card, indigo/emerald glow border) auto-cycling Build/Learn/Bridge sandboxes with a progress bar — already a strength; restyle into the new palette.
   - A slim "three moves" mini-card showing Build / Learn / Bridge with their accent dots.

3. **Trust bar**
   - Mono eyebrow "TRUSTED BY TEAMS ACROSS AFRICA" + logo/name chips on a soft `slate-100` band.

4. **"Why Onebit?" — stepped showcase** *(can be an ink section for contrast)*
   - Centred Sora headline + lead.
   - **Large swapping visual** of the active arm, with three full-width **progress-bar steps** (`01 Build · 02 Learn · 03 Bridge`), each filling in its arm accent as it auto-advances; active step shows label, heading, description, CTA and a stat card.

5. **How it works — three connected cards**
   - Build / Learn / Bridge cards, each headed by its **accent colour** and a node icon; hover = lift + glow border in the arm colour. This is where the three-colour system pays off visually.

6. **Real stories — testimonials**
   - A premium **coverflow carousel** of deep-ink quote cards with indigo/emerald glow, gold quote-mark badge, segmented progress bars. Keep the human proof front-and-centre.

7. **Featured outcome band**
   - Full-width **indigo→emerald gradient** band with a flagship quote + 2–3 metric chips (mono figures).

8. **Build portfolio + "what suits you"** *(ink feature section)*
   - Project cards (real product imagery) with spotlight hover; then two large split cards — **Learn** (emerald) and **Build/Bridge** (indigo/gold) — guiding the two main audiences.

9. **About / closing CTA**
   - Deep-ink panel: "Building technology. Developing people. Connecting opportunity." + dual CTA (gold "Book a Discovery Call" / ghost "Meet the team").

10. **Footer**
    - Ink background, wordmark + one-line mission, arm columns, contact (Abuja), socials, mono fine-print. A faint node-grid watermark.

**Conversion logic:** every screen has exactly **one gold action**; indigo carries navigation and brand; emerald appears only where "learn/growth/positive metric" is the message. This keeps the page energetic but never noisy.

### Why this brief will produce a coherent result
Every decision traces back to the **one idea in the name** and the **three-arm reality of the business**: indigo for engineered intelligence, gold for human opportunity, emerald for growth — unified by an ink neutral and a soft "bit cell" logo, set in wide, friendly, non-tall type. It rebrands Onebit decisively (off teal, new logo, new type) while staying true to what Onebit actually is and does.
